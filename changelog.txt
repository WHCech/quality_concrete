Version: 1.0.0
Date: 25.10.2026
  Major Features:
    - Migrated to Factorio 2.1.
    - Removed the Runtime Spoilage Library dependency. Crafted concrete is now converted the moment a machine or player finishes crafting.
    - Concrete no longer spoils.
  Changes:
    - Recycling items built with concrete (and scrap) gives stone bricks instead of concrete, matching their recipes.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed cycling quality on held concrete. It now switches to the concrete of the next tier, through all qualities of the chain.
    - Fixed machines deleting concrete when their output was not emptied. They now stop with a full output instead.
    - Fixed a load error when replacing concrete gave a recipe the same ingredient twice.
